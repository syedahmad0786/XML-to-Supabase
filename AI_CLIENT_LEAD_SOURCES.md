## 2. Government & Public Databases

### SAM.gov (System for Award Management)
- **URL:** [sam.gov](https://sam.gov)
- **What it is:** Free, centralized source for US government contract opportunities, awards, and registered businesses
- **Search for:** Contract opportunities, registered entities, federal organizations
- **Filters:** NAICS codes, location, socio-economic status, entity name, product/service codes
- **Key pages:**
  - Contract Opportunities: [sam.gov/opportunities](https://sam.gov/opportunities)
  - Contract Data: [sam.gov/contract-data](https://sam.gov/contract-data)
  - Search: [sam.gov/search](https://sam.gov/search)
- **Why useful:** Every business doing federal work is registered here; great for finding established companies

### SBA Dynamic Small Business Search (DSBS)
- **URL:** [search.certifications.sba.gov](https://search.certifications.sba.gov/)
- **What it is:** Database of small businesses registered for federal contracting
- **Search by:** NAICS code, location (state, congressional district), ownership type (minority, veteran, women-owned), keywords
- **Data includes:** Name, location, size, ownership, certifications (8(a), HUBZone, SDVOSB, WOSB)
- **Why useful:** Pre-qualified small businesses with detailed profiles; ideal for finding businesses actively seeking growth

### SBA Open Data Portal
- **URL:** [data.sba.gov](https://data.sba.gov/)
- **What it is:** Open data, reports, and tools from across the SBA

### EDGAR (SEC Database)
- **URL:** [sec.gov/edgar](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar)
- **What it is:** Free public access to corporate financial information and filings for all public companies
- **Why useful:** Research public companies' financials, operations, and strategic priorities to identify AI automation opportunities

## 9. Google Maps & Local Business Scraping

### Why Google Maps is Valuable for Lead Gen
- Contains millions of local business listings with phone numbers, websites, hours, reviews, and categories
- Identifies businesses with weak or no online presence (prime AI automation candidates)
- Geographic segmentation by ZIP code can 3x your results

### Tools for Google Maps Data Extraction

| Tool | URL | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Outscraper** | [outscraper.com](https://outscraper.com/) | Free tier; extracts business data at scale; email enrichment |
| **Apify Google Maps Scraper** | [apify.com](https://apify.com/compass/crawler-google-places) | Bypasses Google's 120-place limit |
| **Scrap.io** | [scrap.io](https://scrap.io) | Google Maps scraping with lead generation focus |
| **Datablist** | [datablist.com](https://www.datablist.com) | Google Maps scraper with CSV export |

### What You Can Extract
- Business name, address, phone number
- Website URL (or lack thereof -- great for identifying businesses needing digital help)
- Business category/industry
- Ratings and review counts
- Operating hours

### Legal Considerations
- Scraping publicly available data is generally permissible under US law
- Factual data (names, addresses, phone numbers) is not copyrightable
- Do NOT scrape/republish user reviews, photos, or business descriptions (copyrighted)
- Respect rate limits and robots.txt guidelines
- Be aware this technically violates Google's Terms of Service

### Pro Tips
- Search by ZIP code rather than city for 2-3x more results
- Target businesses WITHOUT websites (designers/AI agencies can approach them)
- Filter by low review counts or ratings to find businesses needing operational improvements
- Combine with email enrichment tools (Hunter.io, Outscraper) to get contact details

## 12. Strategies for Finding AI Automation Clients

### Strategy 1: Direct Database Prospecting
1. Use **Apollo.io** or **Lusha** (free tiers) to search for decision-makers (CEOs, COOs, VPs of Operations) in target industries
2. Filter by company size (50-500 employees), location, and industry
3. Export contacts and run personalized outreach campaigns

### Strategy 2: Growth Company Targeting
1. Browse the **Inc. 5000** list filtered by industry
2. Cross-reference with **Crunchbase** for recently funded companies
3. These companies often have budget and need to scale with AI

### Strategy 3: Government Contractor Mining
1. Search **SAM.gov** for businesses in your target industry/NAICS codes
2. Cross-reference with **SBA DSBS** for small businesses seeking growth
3. Many government contractors need AI to win more contracts and improve compliance

### Strategy 4: Industry Association Infiltration
1. Find relevant associations via **Directory of Associations** or **Cause IQ**
2. Access their member directories
3. Attend their events and conferences for direct networking

### Strategy 5: Local Business Discovery
1. Use **Google Maps scraping tools** to find businesses in specific areas and industries
2. Target businesses without websites or with poor online presence
3. Enrich contacts with **Hunter.io** or **Outscraper**

### Strategy 6: Trigger-Based Prospecting
1. Set up **Google Alerts** for trigger events: "hiring operations manager," "scaling challenges," "digital transformation"
2. Use **Lead411** or **ZoomInfo** intent data to find companies actively researching AI/automation
3. Monitor **LinkedIn** for companies posting about manual processes or hiring for automatable roles

### Strategy 7: Website Visitor Conversion
1. Set up **Leadfeeder** or **RB2B** on your website
2. Create content about AI automation (blog posts, case studies)
3. Identify companies visiting your site and reach out to decision-makers

### Strategy 8: Review Platform Mining
1. Browse **Clutch.co** for agencies that could benefit from AI (or to list your own services)
2. Check **G2** for companies using manual tools that AI could replace
3. Read **BBB** complaints about slow service or errors -- indicators that AI automation could help

### Key Principles for Outreach
- **Speed matters:** Companies that reach out within 1 hour of initial inquiry are 7x more likely to qualify a lead (Harvard Business Review)
- **Start manual, then automate:** Figure out what outreach works before automating it
- **Use AI for lead scoring:** Hot leads get personal calls; cold leads get automated (but personalized) emails
- **Multi-channel approach:** Combine email, LinkedIn, and phone for best results
- **Personalize based on pain points:** Reference specific industry challenges rather than generic AI benefits
