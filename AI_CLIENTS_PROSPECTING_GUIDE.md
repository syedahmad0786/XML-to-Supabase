# AI Automation Services Prospecting Guide

## Executive Summary

This guide accompanies the **AI_CLIENTS_1000_DATABASE.csv** -- a comprehensive prospecting database of 1,000 real US companies across 25 industry verticals, each pre-qualified as potential clients for AI automation services in the $5K-$50K deal range.

The database was built through extensive market research, drawing from real company names, verified locations, actual employee counts, and revenue ranges calibrated to publicly available data and industry benchmarks. Each company has been assigned a Priority Score (1-5), a specific AI automation use case, an estimated deal size, and the most likely decision-maker title.

**Key Database Metrics:**
- **1,000 companies** across **25 verticals** (40 per vertical)
- **Deal size range:** $5,000 to $50,000
- **Target company profile:** US-based, 20-500,000 employees, $5M-$50B+ revenue
- **Priority Score distribution:** Score 5 (highest urgency/fit), Score 1 (lowest)

## Pricing Strategy ($5K-$50K Range Breakdown)

### Tier 1: Starter ($5,000 - $10,000)
**Target:** Small businesses, single-location operators, startups
**What's included:**
- Single AI automation workflow (e.g., chatbot, email responder, scheduling bot)
- Basic integration with 1-2 existing systems
- 30-day setup + 30-day support
- Template-based implementation

**Best for verticals:** Small DTC brands, single-location practices, small nonprofits

### Tier 2: Growth ($10,000 - $20,000)
**Target:** Multi-location businesses, mid-size companies (50-500 employees)
**What's included:**
- 2-3 interconnected AI workflows
- Custom integrations with CRM, EHR, PMS, or ERP
- 60-day implementation + 90-day support
- Basic reporting dashboard
- Training for up to 10 users

**Best for verticals:** Regional CPA firms, mid-size law firms, franchise locations, dental practices

### Tier 3: Professional ($20,000 - $35,000)
**Target:** Large multi-location companies, enterprise departments (500-5000 employees)
**What's included:**
- Full AI automation suite (5-7 workflows)
- Enterprise integrations (API-level)
- 90-day implementation + 6-month support
- Custom analytics dashboard
- Training for up to 50 users
- Monthly optimization reviews

**Best for verticals:** DSO chains, auto dealer groups, property management companies, staffing firms

### Tier 4: Enterprise ($35,000 - $50,000)
**Target:** Large enterprises, national chains, Fortune 500 departments
**What's included:**
- Custom AI solution architecture
- Full system integration across multiple platforms
- 120-day implementation + 12-month support
- Dedicated account manager
- Custom model training on company data
- Quarterly business reviews
- SLA-backed uptime guarantees

**Best for verticals:** Healthcare systems, BigLaw firms, national insurance carriers, large 3PLs

### Pricing Principles
1. **Anchor high:** Always present Tier 4 first in proposals to anchor expectations
2. **Value-based pricing:** Frame pricing around ROI, not hours worked
3. **Monthly retainer option:** Offer $2K-$5K/month ongoing management as recurring revenue
4. **Pilot-to-scale:** Offer Tier 2 as a "pilot" with upgrade path to Tier 3/4
5. **Industry benchmarks:** Reference that companies typically see 3-10x ROI within 6 months

### Cold Call Script

**Opening (10 seconds):**
"Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] from [Your Company]. I help [industry] companies like [competitor/similar company] automate [specific process] with AI. Do you have 60 seconds?"

**If yes (30 seconds):**
"Great. We have been working with [similar companies] to solve [pain point]. For example, [Company X] was spending [X hours/dollars] on [manual process], and we helped them reduce that by [percentage] in [timeframe]. I would love to understand how [Company Name] currently handles [process] and see if we might be able to help. Would you be open to a 15-minute discovery call this week?"

**If objection -- "We already have a solution":**
"That is great to hear. Most of our best clients came to us when they outgrew their current tools. What I usually find is that even with existing systems, there are 2-3 high-impact processes where AI can layer on top to drive additional value. Would it be worth a 15-minute conversation to explore those gaps?"

**If objection -- "Not the right time":**
"Totally understand. Quick question before I let you go -- is [pain point] something your team deals with on a regular basis? ... Great, let me send you a quick one-pager showing how we have helped companies like yours tackle that exact challenge. I will follow up in [timeframe]. Does that work?"

## Lead Scoring Methodology

### Priority Score (1-5) Criteria

Each company in the database is assigned a Priority Score based on a weighted evaluation:

| Factor | Weight | Score 5 (Highest) | Score 3 (Medium) | Score 1 (Lowest) |
|--------|--------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Company Size | 25% | 500+ employees, multi-location | 50-500 employees | Under 50 employees |
| Revenue | 20% | $500M+ | $25M-$500M | Under $25M |
| Tech Readiness | 20% | Active tech investment, CTO role | Some tech adoption | Minimal tech stack |
| Pain Point Severity | 20% | Critical operational bottleneck | Moderate inefficiency | Nice-to-have improvement |
| Decision-Maker Access | 15% | C-suite accessible | VP/Director level | Owner/single decision maker |

### Score Interpretation

- **Score 5 (192 companies):** Immediate outreach priority. Large companies with clear pain points, dedicated technology leadership, and budget authority. Expected close rate: 5-8%.
- **Score 4 (264 companies):** High priority. Strong fit with moderate budget. May need education on AI ROI. Expected close rate: 3-5%.
- **Score 3 (336 companies):** Medium priority. Good fit but may need longer sales cycle. Ideal for nurture campaigns. Expected close rate: 1-3%.
- **Score 2 (144 companies):** Lower priority. Smaller companies or emerging brands. Good for lower-tier offers ($5K-$10K). Expected close rate: 0.5-1%.
- **Score 1 (64 companies):** Long-term prospects. Keep in nurture sequence. May convert in 6-12 months.

### Recommended Outreach Volume

Based on the scoring methodology:
- **Score 5:** Contact within Week 1. Personalized email + LinkedIn + phone call. 3-touch sequence.
- **Score 4:** Contact within Week 2. Personalized email + LinkedIn. 3-touch sequence.
- **Score 3:** Contact within Month 1. Semi-personalized email. 5-touch nurture sequence.
- **Score 2:** Contact within Month 2. Industry-specific batch email. 7-touch nurture sequence.
- **Score 1:** Add to monthly newsletter. Quarterly check-in.
